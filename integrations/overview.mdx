Hiring doesn't happen in a single tool. Job Dashboard integrates with the platforms your team already uses — from job boards that distribute your openings to thousands of candidates, to calendar apps that keep interviews organized, to messaging tools that keep your team in sync. By connecting these services, you eliminate manual copy-paste work, reduce the risk of missed updates, and give every stakeholder a single place to track hiring progress.

## Available integration categories

### Job boards

Publish open positions directly from Job Dashboard and automatically receive applications in your pipeline. Supported boards include:

- **Indeed** — reach the world's largest job site
- **LinkedIn Jobs** — target professional candidates and leverage LinkedIn's network
- **Glassdoor** — attract candidates who research company culture before applying
- **ZipRecruiter** — distribute postings across a broad network of partner sites

### Calendar & scheduling

Keep interviews on track by syncing with the calendar tools your team already relies on:

- **Google Calendar** — create interview events and share availability directly from a candidate's profile
- **Microsoft Outlook** — sync interview schedules with your organization's Exchange or Microsoft 365 calendar

### Notifications

Stay informed the moment something important happens in your hiring pipeline:

- **Slack** — receive real-time messages in any channel when candidates apply, advance stages, or when interviews are scheduled
- **Email** — configure per-user preferences or team-level rules so the right people are notified at the right time

### Custom & API-based integrations

Build your own connections using Job Dashboard's webhook and REST API infrastructure. Trigger outbound events to any endpoint when pipeline activity occurs, or push data into Job Dashboard from external systems.
