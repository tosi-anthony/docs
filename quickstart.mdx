---

Job Dashboard gives your hiring team everything it needs to attract great candidates, move them through a structured pipeline, and make confident offers — all from a single, intuitive workspace. This guide walks you through signing up, completing your company profile, and publishing your first job posting so applications can start flowing in right away.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [app.jobdashboard.com](https://app.jobdashboard.com) and click **Get Started Free**. Enter your work email address and choose a strong password, then click **Create Account**.

    Prefer a faster path? Click **Continue with Google** to sign up using your Google Workspace credentials — no password required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for an email from **no-reply@jobdashboard.com** with the subject line *Confirm your Job Dashboard account*. Click the **Verify Email** button inside the message.

    <Note>
      The verification link expires after 24 hours. If you don't see the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification email** on the confirmation screen.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your company profile">
    After verifying your email, Job Dashboard prompts you to set up your organization. Fill in the following fields:

    - **Company name** — the legal or brand name shown on all public job posts.
    - **Industry** — used to surface your listings to relevant candidates.
    - **Company size** — helps candidates understand the scale of your team.
    - **Website URL** — links candidates back to your main site.
    - **Company logo** — upload a PNG or SVG (minimum 200 × 200 px) for a polished public presence.
    - **Company bio** — write two to four sentences describing your mission, culture, and what makes your team unique.

    Click **Save & Continue** when you're done.

    <Tip>
      You can invite your colleagues now so they can contribute to the profile and help review applications from day one. Head to **Settings → Team** and enter their email addresses. Job Dashboard supports three roles: Admin, Hiring Manager, and Interviewer — assign whichever fits each person's responsibilities.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first job posting">
    From the main navigation, click **Jobs → New Job**. Complete each field in the job editor:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Job title** | The role name candidates will see, e.g., *Senior Product Designer*. |
    | **Description** | Full role overview — responsibilities, requirements, and what success looks like. Markdown formatting is supported. |
    | **Location** | Office address, city, or *Remote* / *Hybrid*. |
    | **Employment type** | Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, or Freelance. |
    | **Salary range** | Optional but strongly recommended — postings with salary ranges receive significantly more qualified applicants. |

    Work through each section at your own pace; Job Dashboard saves a draft automatically every 30 seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish the job">
    When you're satisfied with the posting, click **Publish Job** in the top-right corner of the editor. Job Dashboard will:

    1. Run a completeness check to flag any missing recommended fields.
    2. Make the posting publicly visible on your company's Job Dashboard careers page.
    3. Generate a shareable link you can distribute on LinkedIn, your website, or anywhere else you recruit.

    Your new job now appears under **Jobs → Active** with a status badge of **Live**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View incoming applications in the pipeline">
    Click the job title from your **Jobs** list to open the role's pipeline view. As candidates apply, their cards appear in the **Applied** column. Drag a card to the right to advance them through your stages — for example, **Phone Screen → Interview → Offer**.

    Each candidate card shows:

    - Resume and cover letter attachments
    - Application date and source
    - Star rating and internal notes from your team
    - A timeline of every action taken on the application

    Use the **Filters** panel on the left to narrow the pipeline by stage, rating, location, or application date.
  </Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
  If you want to automate job posting, trigger pipeline actions from your own systems, or sync candidate data with an ATS, explore the [Job Dashboard API Reference](/api/overview). The REST API supports every action available in the UI.
</Note>
