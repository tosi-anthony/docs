## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.jobdashboard.com](https://app.jobdashboard.com) and verify your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    Complete your company profile and invite your team members from **Settings → Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post your first job">
    Go to **Jobs → New Job**, fill in the details, and publish it to your connected job boards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track applications">
    Review incoming applications in your pipeline and move candidates through hiring stages.
  </Step>
</Steps>
