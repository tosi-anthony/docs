The Jobs section is your central workspace for every open, draft, paused, and closed role at your company. From here you can spin up new postings in minutes, keep existing listings accurate, and retire roles once hiring wraps up — all without leaving Job Dashboard.

## The Jobs List View

When you open **Jobs** from the left-hand navigation, you land on a table that shows every job your organization has created. Each row contains the following columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The job title as it appears on the posting. Click to open the job detail page. |
| **Department** | The team or business unit the role belongs to. |
| **Location** | The office location or "Remote" / "Hybrid" designation. |
| **Status** | One of `Draft`, `Published`, `Paused`, or `Closed`. |
| **Applicants** | Total number of candidates who have applied to this role. |
| **Posted Date** | The date the job was first published externally. |

## Filtering and Searching Jobs

Use the toolbar above the table to narrow the list to the jobs you care about.

- **Search bar** — Type any part of a job title to filter rows in real time.
- **Status filter** — Select one or more statuses (`Draft`, `Published`, `Paused`, `Closed`) from the dropdown.
- **Department filter** — Limit the view to a single department or a custom selection.
- **Location filter** — Filter by office location or remote/hybrid work type.

To clear all active filters at once, click **Reset filters** on the right side of the toolbar.

## Creating a New Job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new job form">
    Click **+ New Job** in the top-right corner of the Jobs page. The job creation form opens in a full-page view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Complete the required fields in the **Details** section:

    - **Job Title** — The public-facing title candidates will see.
    - **Department** — Choose from your configured departments, or add a new one inline.
    - **Location** — Select an office location or choose Remote / Hybrid.
    - **Employment Type** — Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship.
    - **Salary Range** *(optional)* — Enter a minimum and maximum and select the currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the job description">
    Use the rich-text editor in the **Description** section to write the role summary, responsibilities, and requirements. You can paste from an external editor — formatting is preserved automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the hiring pipeline">
    In the **Pipeline** section, choose which hiring stages apply to this role. You can use your default pipeline or customise the stages specifically for this job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the application form">
    In the **Application Form** section, select which fields candidates must complete when they apply. Toggle optional fields on or off as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or publish">
    Choose how to proceed (see [Saving as Draft vs. Publishing](#saving-as-draft-vs-publishing-immediately) below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving as Draft vs. Publishing Immediately

At the bottom of the new job form you have two primary actions:

- **Save as Draft** — Saves the job without making it visible to candidates. Use this when the posting is not yet ready for review or approval. Draft jobs appear in the Jobs list with a `Draft` badge and are never distributed to job boards.
- **Publish** — Makes the job live immediately. The posting becomes visible on your careers page and is queued for distribution to any connected job boards.

You can return to a draft at any time by clicking the job title in the list and resuming where you left off.

## Editing a Published Job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Click the job title in the Jobs list to open the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Click **Edit Job** in the top-right corner of the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update any fields — title, description, salary range, pipeline stages, or application form questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. The updated posting is pushed to your careers page and queued for sync to connected job boards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changes to a published job may take up to 15 minutes to sync to connected job boards. Your careers page updates immediately, but external listings (Indeed, LinkedIn, etc.) reflect changes once the sync cycle completes.
</Note>

## Pausing or Closing a Job

**Pausing** a job temporarily stops new applications from being accepted while keeping the posting visible. **Closing** a job removes it from your careers page entirely and marks it as inactive.

To pause or close a job:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the job">
    Find the job in the Jobs list or open its detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the status menu">
    Click the **⋯** (more options) icon at the end of the row, or use the **Status** dropdown inside the detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new status">
    Choose **Pause Job** or **Close Job** from the menu. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can reopen a paused or closed job at any time by returning to this menu and selecting **Republish**.

## Using Job Templates to Speed Up Posting

Job templates let you save a fully configured job — description, pipeline, and application form — so you can reuse it for future openings without starting from scratch.

**To save a job as a template:**

1. Open the job detail page.
2. Click **⋯ → Save as Template**.
3. Give the template a name and click **Save**.

**To create a new job from a template:**

1. Click **+ New Job**.
2. Select **Start from template** at the top of the form.
3. Choose a template from the list — all fields pre-populate.
4. Make any role-specific edits, then save as draft or publish.

## Bulk Actions

You can act on multiple jobs at once from the Jobs list:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select jobs">
    Check the box on the left side of each row you want to include. Check the box in the table header to select all visible rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the bulk actions menu">
    A toolbar appears at the bottom of the page showing the number of selected jobs. Click **Actions** to open the bulk actions menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Select one of the available bulk actions:

    - **Change Status** — Move selected jobs to Draft, Published, Paused, or Closed.
    - **Delete** — Permanently delete selected jobs. This action cannot be undone; all associated applications will also be removed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the confirmation dialog and click **Confirm** to apply the action.
  </Step>
</Steps>
