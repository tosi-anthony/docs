The Candidates section gives you a unified view of every person in your talent pool — whether they submitted a formal application, were sourced by your team, or were added manually. From one place you can review resumes, leave feedback, track communication history, and manage duplicate records to keep your database clean.

## The Candidates List View

Navigating to **Candidates** in the left-hand menu shows a table of every candidate record in your organization's account. The table includes the following columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The candidate's full name. Click to open their profile. |
| **Email** | Primary email address on file. |
| **Applied Jobs** | The number of roles this candidate has applied to. |
| **Stage** | The current pipeline stage across their most recent active application. |
| **Source** | How the candidate entered your system (e.g., Indeed, LinkedIn, Referral, Direct). |
| **Date Added** | The date the candidate record was first created. |

## Searching and Filtering Candidates

Use the toolbar above the candidates table to zero in on the people you need.

- **Search bar** — Search by name or email address. Results update as you type.
- **Stage filter** — Filter by one or more pipeline stages (e.g., Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer).
- **Source filter** — Narrow the list to candidates from a specific acquisition channel.
- **Tag filter** — Show only candidates who have been assigned a particular tag.
- **Date range filter** — Limit results to candidates added within a custom date window. Click the date field to open the calendar picker and set a start and end date.

To remove all active filters, click **Clear all** on the right side of the filter bar.

## Opening a Candidate Profile

Click any candidate's name in the list to open their full profile. The profile is split into several sections:

- **Overview** — Contact details, source, tags, and a summary of active applications.
- **Resume / CV** — The uploaded resume renders inline; click **Download** to save a copy.
- **Applications** — A timeline of every job this candidate has applied to, with their current stage in each pipeline.
- **Notes** — Team-visible notes and structured feedback entries.
- **Emails** — A log of all emails sent to or received from this candidate via Job Dashboard.
- **Activity** — A chronological audit trail of every action taken on the profile.

## Adding Notes and Feedback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the candidate profile">
    Click the candidate's name to open their profile, then select the **Notes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new note">
    Click **+ Add Note**. A text editor appears where you can write free-form notes about the candidate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally attach a rating">
    Use the star rating selector (1–5) to attach a structured quality signal to your note. This rating rolls up into the candidate's overall feedback summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the note">
    Click **Save Note**. The note is immediately visible to all team members with access to the role. Notes are timestamped and attributed to the author.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To edit or delete a note you authored, click the **⋯** icon next to the note and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

## Tagging Candidates

Tags let you group and filter candidates by custom criteria — for example, `Strong Pipeline`, `Future Role`, or `Requires Visa Sponsorship`.

**To add a tag:**

1. Open the candidate profile.
2. In the **Overview** section, click **+ Add Tag** next to the Tags field.
3. Type to search existing tags or enter a new tag name and press **Enter** to create it.
4. The tag saves immediately and appears in the list view's Tag filter.

**To remove a tag:** Click the **×** on the tag badge inside the profile.

## Manually Adding a Candidate

You can add a candidate to your database without them submitting a formal application — useful for sourced candidates or referrals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the new candidate form">
    Click **+ Add Candidate** in the top-right corner of the Candidates page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter contact details">
    Fill in the candidate's **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**. These are the only required fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional details">
    Optionally provide a phone number, LinkedIn URL, source, and any initial tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a resume">
    Click **Upload Resume** to attach a PDF or Word document directly to the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link to a job (optional)">
    Toggle **Add to a job** to associate this candidate with an open role and place them at a specific pipeline stage right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save Candidate**. The new profile appears in the Candidates list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Merging Duplicate Candidate Records

If the same person has two separate profiles (for example, from applying through different channels), you can merge them into a single record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the primary profile">
    Navigate to the candidate profile you want to keep as the primary record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a merge">
    Click **⋯ → Merge Duplicate** in the top-right corner of the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the duplicate">
    Type the duplicate candidate's name or email in the search field. Select them from the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the merge preview">
    Job Dashboard shows a side-by-side comparison of both records. Confirm which profile fields (name, email, phone) to keep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the merge">
    Click **Merge**. All applications, notes, emails, and activity from the secondary record are transferred to the primary profile, and the duplicate is deleted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exporting Candidate Data

To export candidate records as a CSV file:

1. Apply any filters or search terms to scope the export to the candidates you need.
2. Click **Export** in the top-right corner of the Candidates page.
3. Choose **Export current view** to export only filtered results, or **Export all candidates** for a full dump.
4. Click **Download CSV**. The file downloads to your browser immediately.

<Warning>
  Exported CSVs contain personal data including names, email addresses, and application history. Handle all exported files in accordance with your organization's privacy policy and applicable data protection regulations (such as GDPR or CCPA). Do not share exports via unsecured channels or store them beyond their necessary retention period.
</Warning>
