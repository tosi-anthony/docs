Your billing settings are the single place to review your current plan, compare available tiers, update your payment method, and download invoices for your records. Changes to your subscription take effect immediately, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

<Note>
  Only users with the **Admin** role can view or make changes in Billing settings. If you need to update your plan or payment details and don't have Admin access, ask your organization's Admin.
</Note>

## Available plans

Job Dashboard offers four plans to match teams at different stages of growth.

| Feature | Free | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active job postings | 3 | 10 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Team members | 2 | 5 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Job board integrations | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Slack notifications | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Analytics & reports | Basic | Standard | Advanced | Custom |
| API access | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Dedicated support | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Custom SSO (SAML) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| SLA guarantee | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

- **Free** — designed for small teams posting their first roles. Good for getting started with no commitment.
- **Starter** — adds job board integrations and Slack notifications for growing teams handling multiple roles at once.
- **Growth** — removes posting and seat limits and unlocks advanced analytics and API access for teams hiring at scale.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, unlimited seats, dedicated support, SAML SSO, and an uptime SLA for large organizations with complex requirements. [Contact sales](mailto:sales@jobdashboard.io) to discuss Enterprise.

<Tip>
  Switch to annual billing and save 20% compared to paying month-to-month. You can toggle between monthly and annual billing at any time from **Settings → Billing → Plan**. If you switch mid-cycle, we prorate the difference automatically.
</Tip>

## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Select the plan you want to move to and choose monthly or annual billing.
4. Review the pricing summary and click **Confirm change**.

Upgrades take effect immediately — you gain access to new features right away and are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period so you keep your current features until you've used what you paid for.

If a downgrade would put you over the new plan's limits (for example, you have 12 active jobs but are downgrading to Starter's limit of 10), Job Dashboard will prompt you to archive the excess jobs before confirming.

## Manage payment methods

Job Dashboard accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).

**To add or update a card:**

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.
2. Click **Add payment method** or **Replace card**.
3. Enter your card details in the secure form and click **Save**.

**To remove a card:**

1. Click the three-dot menu next to the card and select **Remove**.
2. Confirm in the dialog.

You must always have at least one payment method on file while on a paid plan.

## Download invoices

Job Dashboard generates a PDF invoice for every successful payment.

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
2. Find the invoice you need in the list (sorted by date, most recent first).
3. Click **Download PDF** to save a copy.

Invoices include your organization name, billing address, VAT/tax ID (if configured), itemized charges, and payment status. To update the name or address that appears on invoices, click **Edit billing details** at the top of the Invoices page.

## Cancel your subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Select a cancellation reason (optional) and click **Confirm cancellation**.

Your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period. You won't be charged again after that date.

### What happens to your data after cancellation

After your paid subscription ends, your account moves to the Free plan. If your current usage exceeds Free plan limits (for example, more than 3 active jobs or more than 2 team members), the excess jobs are automatically archived and additional team members lose access — they are not permanently deleted.

Job Dashboard retains your data for **90 days** after your account drops to Free. During that window, you can reactivate a paid plan at any time to restore full access. After 90 days of inactivity on a Free account, data may be permanently deleted in accordance with our [data retention policy](https://jobdashboard.io/legal/data-retention).

<Warning>
  Cancelling your subscription does not immediately delete your data, but permanently closing your account does. If you want your data removed before the 90-day retention period ends, go to **Settings → Profile → Account** and select **Delete organization**.
</Warning>
