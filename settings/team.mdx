Team settings give Admins full control over who has access to your Job Dashboard organization and what they can do once they're in. You can invite new members by email, assign them a role that matches their responsibilities, change roles as people's responsibilities evolve, and revoke access when team members move on.

## Invite a new team member

1. Go to **Settings → Team** and click **Invite member**.
2. Enter the person's work email address.
3. Select the role you want to assign them (see the roles table below).
4. Click **Send invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a link to create their account. The invite link expires after 72 hours. If it expires, return to **Settings → Team**, find the pending invite, and click **Resend invite**.

## Available roles and permissions

Job Dashboard has three built-in roles. Choose the one that best matches each team member's level of responsibility.

| Permission | Admin | Hiring Manager | Interviewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post and edit jobs | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Close and archive jobs | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| View all candidates | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ (assigned jobs only) |
| Move candidates through stages | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Submit interview feedback | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| View interview feedback from others | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Send offers | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| View reports and analytics | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage team members and roles | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Manage integrations | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Access billing settings | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Transfer organization ownership | ✅ (owner only) | ❌ | ❌ |

- **Admin** — full access to all features, settings, and billing. Assign this role sparingly.
- **Hiring Manager** — can manage jobs, move candidates through the pipeline, and view analytics, but cannot change team settings or billing.
- **Interviewer** — limited to viewing candidates on jobs they're assigned to and submitting their own feedback. Ideal for people who participate in interviews but don't manage the hiring process.

## Change a member's role

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the team member in the list and click **Edit** (or the three-dot menu) next to their name.
3. Select the new role from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save**.

The change takes effect immediately. The member's existing sessions are updated without requiring them to log out.

## Remove a team member

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the team member and click the three-dot menu next to their name.
3. Select **Remove from organization**.
4. Confirm your decision in the dialog.

The member loses access immediately. Any open browser sessions they have are invalidated within a few minutes.

<Warning>
  Removing a team member does not delete their activity history. Their interview feedback, candidate notes, and stage-change actions remain part of the hiring record and continue to be visible to Admins and Hiring Managers. Their name is preserved in historical records to maintain an accurate audit trail.
</Warning>

## Transfer organization ownership

The organization owner has exclusive access to transfer ownership and certain irreversible billing actions. To transfer ownership to another Admin:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Click the three-dot menu next to the Admin you want to make the new owner.
3. Select **Transfer ownership**.
4. Confirm by entering your password.

After the transfer, your role changes to Admin. The new owner gains sole access to the ownership-only actions. Only one person can be the organization owner at a time.
