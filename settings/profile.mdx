Your profile settings control how you appear to teammates inside Job Dashboard and how you authenticate to your account. You can update your personal details, connect a single sign-on provider, set your timezone, and fine-tune which hiring events send you an email — all from **Settings → Profile**.

## Update your display name and profile photo

Your display name appears on candidate notes, interview feedback, and activity logs visible to your team.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile**.
2. Click your current avatar or the **Upload photo** button to choose an image from your device. Photos must be JPEG or PNG and under 5 MB.
3. Edit the **Display name** field with the name you'd like teammates to see.
4. Click **Save changes**.

## Change your email address

Your email address is used for login and for all notification emails from Job Dashboard.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile** and click **Edit** next to your current email.
2. Enter your new email address and click **Send verification email**.
3. Open the verification email sent to your new address and click the confirmation link.

Your email address updates only after you confirm the link. Until then, your old address remains active.

## Change your password

1. Go to **Settings → Profile → Security**.
2. Click **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
4. Click **Update password**.

If you've forgotten your current password, sign out and use the **Forgot password** link on the login page instead.

## Connect Google SSO

Connecting Google SSO lets you sign in to Job Dashboard with your Google account instead of a password.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile → Security**.
2. Click **Connect Google account**.
3. Complete the Google authorization flow in the popup window.

Once connected, you can use **Continue with Google** on the login page. Your password login continues to work alongside SSO unless your organization's Admin has enforced SSO-only access.

## Set timezone and language preferences

Job Dashboard displays all interview times, deadline dates, and activity timestamps in your local timezone.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile → Preferences**.
2. Select your timezone from the **Timezone** dropdown. Start typing your city or UTC offset to filter the list.
3. Select your preferred language from the **Language** dropdown.
4. Click **Save preferences**.

## Manage personal email notification preferences

You control which hiring events send you an email without affecting what your teammates receive.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile → Notification preferences**.
2. Toggle individual events on or off. See the [Notifications integration guide](/integrations/notifications) for the full list of available events.
3. Click **Save preferences**.

<Note>
  Team-level notification rules set by your Admin may send emails to you even if you've turned off a preference personally, for certain high-priority events. Contact your Admin if you want to adjust those rules.
</Note>

## Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your personal data and revokes your access to your organization's Job Dashboard workspace.

1. Go to **Settings → Profile → Account**.
2. Scroll to the **Danger zone** section and click **Delete my account**.
3. Type your email address to confirm and click **Permanently delete account**.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is irreversible. Your activity history — including interview feedback, candidate notes, and stage changes — remains visible to your organization because it is part of the hiring record, but your name will be anonymized. If you are the sole Admin of your organization, you must transfer ownership before you can delete your account.
</Warning>
