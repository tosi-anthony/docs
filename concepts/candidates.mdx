Every person who applies to one of your jobs becomes a **Candidate** in Job Dashboard. A candidate record is a single, persistent profile that follows that person across your entire organization — regardless of how many roles they have applied for, how much time has passed, or which team member is handling the review. Centralizing this information means you never lose context, and you can make faster, better-informed hiring decisions.

## What a candidate record contains

### Personal information

The core identity fields on every candidate profile are:

- **Name** — First and last name, automatically parsed from the application form or resume.
- **Email address** — The primary contact address. All automated and manual emails are sent here.
- **Phone number** — Optional. Stored for interview scheduling and direct outreach.
- **LinkedIn URL** — Optional. Link directly to the candidate's public profile for additional context.

### Resume / CV

Candidates can upload a resume during the application process, or you can attach one manually when adding a candidate directly. Job Dashboard stores the original file and renders a preview inline on the profile page. You can upload multiple versions if a candidate resubmits — all versions are retained and timestamped.

### Tags

Tags let you label candidates for quick filtering across your database. Use them however fits your workflow — for example, `strong-portfolio`, `senior`, `referred`, or `pipeline-2025`. You can apply multiple tags to a single candidate and filter the candidate list by any combination of tags.

### Notes and interview feedback

Your team can leave free-text notes on any candidate profile. Interview feedback is structured separately — when a team member completes an interview stage, they submit a feedback form with a rating and written comments. All notes and feedback are visible to everyone with access to that role, so every reviewer starts fully informed.

### Activity history

The **Activity** tab on every candidate profile is an automatic, chronological log of everything that has happened, including:

- Emails sent or received through Job Dashboard
- Stage changes across all applications
- Notes added by team members
- Status changes (e.g., marked as hired or rejected)
- Resume uploads and updates

You cannot edit or delete entries in the activity history — it is an immutable audit trail.

## One candidate, multiple applications

A candidate in Job Dashboard is separate from any individual application. If the same person applies to two different jobs — or if you manually add them to a second role — Job Dashboard links both applications to the same candidate profile. You can see all of their active and past applications from a single view, and notes or tags you add at the candidate level are visible across all of their applications.

<Note>
  Job Dashboard stores candidate data in accordance with the retention periods
  you configure. To review or update your data retention rules, anonymization
  schedules, and candidate deletion workflows, go to **Settings → Privacy**.
  If you operate in the EU or UK, ensure your retention settings comply with
  GDPR requirements before collecting candidate data.
</Note>
