A **Job** is the fundamental unit of your hiring activity in Job Dashboard. Every candidate application, every pipeline stage, and every recruitment metric traces back to a specific job record. When you create a job, you are not just writing a posting — you are configuring a workspace that organizes all the activity around filling that role.

## Key fields

When you create or edit a job, you fill in the following fields:

- **Title** — The position name candidates will see (e.g., *Senior Product Designer*).
- **Description** — A rich-text field for responsibilities, requirements, and your company pitch. This is what appears on connected job boards.
- **Location** — Choose **Remote**, **On-site**, or **Hybrid**. For on-site and hybrid roles, specify a city and country.
- **Employment type** — **Full-time**, **Part-time**, or **Contract**.
- **Salary range** — Optional. Displaying a range improves applicant quality and is required in some jurisdictions.
- **Department** — Assign the job to a department (e.g., Engineering, Marketing) for reporting and filtering.
- **Closing date** — The date after which the job will stop accepting new applications. Leave blank to keep the role open indefinitely.

<Tip>
  Keep your job description under 700 words. Postings that are concise and
  scannable consistently attract more qualified applicants than lengthy,
  exhaustive lists of requirements.
</Tip>

## Job statuses

Every job is always in one of four statuses. You can change a job's status at any time from the job detail page.

| Status | What it means |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Draft** | The job is being written or reviewed. It is not visible to candidates anywhere and has not been sent to any job boards. |
| **Published** | The job is live. It is visible to candidates on your careers page and any connected external job boards. |
| **Paused** | The job is temporarily hidden from external listings. Existing applications continue to move through the pipeline, but no new applications are accepted. |
| **Closed** | The role has been filled or cancelled. The job is archived, no new applications are accepted, and it is removed from all job board listings. |

## How jobs appear on connected job boards

When you publish a job, Job Dashboard automatically syncs it to every job board you have connected under **Settings → Integrations → Job Boards**. The job title, description, location, employment type, and salary range (if provided) are pushed to each board. Updates you make to the job in Job Dashboard propagate to connected boards within a few minutes.

If you want to publish to only a subset of your connected boards, open the job, navigate to the **Distribution** tab, and toggle individual boards on or off before publishing.

## Internal vs. external visibility

Some roles should not be advertised publicly — a confidential backfill or an internal-only promotion, for example. Job Dashboard gives you two visibility settings:

- **External** — The default. The job is visible on your public careers page and synced to external job boards when published.
- **Internal** — The job appears only on your internal careers portal (if configured) and is never synced to external boards. Share the direct link with employees to collect referrals or internal applications.

You can switch between Internal and External at any time, even after a job is published. Switching a live external job to Internal immediately removes it from all external boards.
