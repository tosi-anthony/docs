Job Dashboard is built around four interconnected entities: **Jobs**, **Candidates**, **Applications**, and the **Pipeline**. Think of it like a funnel — you open a position (a Job), people express interest (becoming Candidates), each expression of interest becomes a tracked record (an Application), and every application travels through a series of steps toward a hiring decision (the Pipeline). Understanding how these pieces relate to each other will help you get the most out of every feature in the platform.

## How the entities relate

A **Job** represents a single open position at your company. When someone applies to that job, Job Dashboard creates an **Application** linking that person to that role. The person themselves becomes a **Candidate** — a persistent profile that exists independently of any single application. This means one candidate can apply to multiple jobs, and you can track all of their applications from a single profile. Each application moves through your **Pipeline**, a configurable sequence of stages that reflects your real-world hiring process, from initial screening all the way to an offer or rejection.

Here is a quick mental model:

- A **Job** is the role you are hiring for.
- A **Candidate** is the person who wants to fill it.
- An **Application** is the relationship between the two.
- The **Pipeline** is the journey that application takes.
