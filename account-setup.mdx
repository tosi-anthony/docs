---
title: "How to Set Up Your Job Dashboard Account and Hiring Team"
sidebarTitle: "Account Setup"
description: "Configure your Job Dashboard organization, customize your company profile, and invite your hiring team before you post your first job."
---

Before you publish your first job posting, a few minutes of setup will pay dividends across every hire you make. Job Dashboard's organization settings control everything candidates see on your public careers page, how your team collaborates in the pipeline, and which notifications keep the right people informed at the right time. Follow the steps below to get your account fully configured and your hiring team ready to go.

<Steps>

  <Step title="Sign up for Job Dashboard">
    Go to [app.jobdashboard.com](https://app.jobdashboard.com) and choose how you'd like to register:

    - **Email and password** — enter your work email, choose a password of at least 10 characters, and click **Create Account**.
    - **Google SSO** — click **Continue with Google** and select your Google Workspace account. No password is created; Google handles authentication on every subsequent sign-in.

    Either method gives you a fully functional account with a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan — no credit card required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Job Dashboard sends a verification email to the address you registered with. Open the email from **no-reply@jobdashboard.com** and click **Verify Email**.

    <Note>
      If you signed up with Google SSO, your email is already verified automatically and you can skip this step.
    </Note>

    Completing verification unlocks the ability to publish jobs and invite team members. The verification link is valid for 24 hours; use the **Resend verification email** link on the confirmation screen if you need a fresh one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create and name your organization">
    After verifying your email, Job Dashboard prompts you to create your organization — the top-level container for all your jobs, candidates, and team members.

    1. Enter your **Organization name**. This is typically your company's legal name or the brand name candidates will recognize.
    2. Choose an **Organization URL slug** (e.g., `acme`). Your public careers page will live at `jobs.jobdashboard.com/acme`.
    3. Select your **Industry** and **Company size** from the dropdowns.
    4. Click **Create Organization**.

    You can rename your organization later under **Settings → Organization**, but changing the URL slug will break any previously shared links to your careers page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your company logo and write your company bio">
    Your logo and bio appear on every public job posting and your careers page, so candidates form their first impression here.

    **To upload your logo:**

    1. Go to **Settings → Company Profile**.
    2. Click the logo placeholder and select an image file. Job Dashboard accepts PNG, JPG, or SVG files up to 5 MB. For best results, use a square image of at least 400 × 400 px.
    3. Use the crop tool to adjust framing, then click **Save**.

    **To write your company bio:**

    In the **About** field on the same page, write two to five sentences that capture your company's mission, product or service, culture, and what makes working there compelling. This copy is indexed by search engines, so natural, descriptive language works better than buzzwords.

    Click **Save Profile** when you're finished.

    <Tip>
      Profiles with a logo and a bio of at least 150 characters receive up to 40% more applicant engagement than incomplete profiles. Take a few extra minutes here — it's worth it.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite team members and assign roles">
    Bring your hiring team into Job Dashboard so everyone can collaborate on pipeline reviews and interviews.

    1. Navigate to **Settings → Team**.
    2. Click **Invite Member**.
    3. Enter the person's work email address and select their role.
    4. Click **Send Invite**. They'll receive an email with a link to join your organization.

    Job Dashboard includes three built-in roles:

    | Role | Permissions |
    |---|---|
    | **Admin** | Full access: manage billing, delete the organization, configure settings, create and publish jobs, move candidates, and invite or remove team members. |
    | **Hiring Manager** | Create and edit job postings, manage the candidate pipeline, leave notes, schedule interviews, and view analytics for their assigned jobs. Cannot access billing or organization settings. |
    | **Interviewer** | View candidate profiles and scorecards for jobs they've been assigned to, submit interview feedback, and leave notes. Cannot create jobs, move candidates, or view other jobs. |

    <Warning>
      Only **Admins** can manage your subscription, update payment details, and permanently delete the organization. Deleting the organization is irreversible and removes all jobs, candidates, and pipeline data. Assign the Admin role only to people who need it.
    </Warning>

    You can change a member's role at any time from **Settings → Team** by clicking the three-dot menu next to their name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your default timezone and notification preferences">
    Correct timezone settings ensure that interview scheduling, deadline reminders, and activity timestamps all display accurately for your team.

    **To set your timezone:**

    1. Go to **Settings → Organization → General**.
    2. Select your timezone from the **Default timezone** dropdown. Individual team members can override this for their own account under **My Profile → Preferences**.
    3. Click **Save**.

    **To configure notifications:**

    1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** (organization-wide defaults) or **My Profile → Notifications** (personal preferences).
    2. Toggle the events you want to be notified about:

       - New application received
       - Candidate stage changed
       - Interview scheduled or cancelled
       - Team member leaves a note
       - Job posting expires

    3. Choose your delivery method — **Email**, **In-app**, or both — for each event type.
    4. Click **Save Preferences**.

    <Tip>
      If your team uses Slack, connect the Job Dashboard Slack integration under **Settings → Integrations** to receive pipeline updates directly in your hiring channels.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

</Steps>

With your organization configured, your team invited, and notifications tuned, you're ready to create your first job posting. Head over to the [Quick Start guide](/quickstart) to publish a live role in under five minutes.
